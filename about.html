ABOUT PRIME FOCUS SCHOOLS

A foundation for lifelong learning.

Prime Focus Schools is an educational institution located in Ipaja, Lagos State, Nigeria. The school is committed to providing quality education that promotes academic excellence, moral values, discipline, creativity, and personal development.

Beyond classroom learning, Prime Focus Schools places importance on practical education and vocational skills, equipping students with valuable knowledge and abilities for everyday life and future opportunities. The vocational skills offered include fashion designing, barbing, music, hairdressing, gele tying, makeup artistry, and computer skills.

Through its combination of academic education and vocational training, the school encourages students to discover their talents, develop practical skills, explore their interests, and become confident and productive individuals. Prime Focus Schools aims to nurture well-rounded learners who can contribute positively to society and prepare themselves for a successful future.

We value the partnership between students, parents, teachers and the wider school community in supporting every learner.
